# 🧑‍💼 HR Analytics Dashboard

This project presents a **comprehensive HR Analytics Dashboard** built in Excel to analyze employee attrition, demographic trends, departmental performance, and business risks related to workforce turnover.  
Using **Power Query, Pivot Tables, DAX-like logic in Excel, interactive slicers, and dynamic KPIs**, the dashboard helps HR teams understand *why employees leave* and *how retention can be improved*.

The analysis is supported with a **detailed presentation (PPT)** covering insights, business problems, and strategic solutions.

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

- **Total Employees:** 1470  
- **Current Employees:** 1233 (84%)  
- **Attrition:** 237 (16%)  
- **Average Monthly Income:** 6503  
- **Average Age:** 36 Years  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Employees aged **25–34** recorded the highest attrition (**112 exits**)  
- **R&D department** has the maximum attrition (**133 exits**)  
- **Sales** department has the second-highest attrition (**92 exits**)  
- Roles most affected by attrition:  
  - Laboratory Technician  
  - Research Scientist  
  - Sales Executive  
- **Male attrition (63%)** is higher than **female attrition (37%)**

---

## 🚨 Business Problems Identified

1. High attrition among **young and talented employees**  
2. **Male employees** leaving more frequently  
3. Extremely high attrition in the **R&D department**  
4. **Sales Executives** experiencing major turnover  

---

## 🛠 Proposed Solutions – Career Growth

- Define a **clear promotion roadmap**  
- Launch **Learning & Development Programs**  
- Provide **skill-based certifications** & training initiatives  

---

## 🎖 Solutions – Incentives & Recognition

- Introduce **target-based bonuses**  
- Launch recognition programs such as:  
  - *Employee of the Month* awards  
  - Public appreciation in newsletters/events  
  - Gift vouchers & additional holidays  
- Motivate employees by valuing their efforts  

---

## 🧘‍♂️ Solutions – Work-Life Balance

- Provide **flexible timings** or remote work options  
- Conduct **wellness programs** (gym, health checkups, counseling)  
- Reduce stress to improve retention  

---

## 🗣 Solutions – Engagement & Feedback

- Conduct **regular employee feedback surveys**  
- Use **exit interviews** for understanding real causes  
- Immediately address employee concerns  
- Build a culture of open communication  

---

## ✅ Closing Summary 

- Highest attrition is among **young & talented employees**  
- **R&D and Sales departments** require urgent intervention  
- With the right mix of **career growth, recognition, work-life balance, and feedback systems**, attrition can be significantly reduced  
- **“Happy & motivated employees = Strong company future.”**
